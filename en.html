Health and care for people who matter to each other.

LifeMate is not a single app. It is the core of an extensible ecosystem. WellMate and CareMate are its first public products: one for managing your own health routines, the other for consent-based support from someone you trust.

LifeMate products

Two distinct experiences, one shared philosophy.

WellMate · Personal health

Keep everyday health routines from becoming scattered or easy to miss.

WellMate helps you manage medication, reminders, health calendar items and everyday health events in one clear personal space.

  • Keep routines and reminders organized.
  • Log everyday health information simply.
  • Share only selected information, if you choose.
WellMate launch status →
CareMate · Trusted care

Care without turning support into control.

CareMate is for someone you trust. They can see only the information you have allowed and be there when their support is useful.

  • Stay informed about approved, important updates.
  • Support at the right time instead of constant monitoring.
  • Respect privacy boundaries and individual autonomy.

How it works

Three steps, always on your terms.

1

Manage your own health

Keep routines and everyday information in WellMate.

2

Connect someone you trust, if you want

The individual chooses who can be connected.

3

Only approved, necessary information flows

CareMate sees only what has been selected for sharing.

Privacy & trust

Good care needs clear boundaries.

LifeMate is designed around consent and user control. Access should be understandable, changeable and limited to what is actually needed.

Read privacy principles
✓

Consent-based

Connection and sharing start with the individual’s choice.

✓

Transparent

People should understand what is shared and with whom.

✓

Controllable

Access levels should remain manageable and changeable.

✓

Minimum necessary access

The goal is relevant information, not broader access than needed.
