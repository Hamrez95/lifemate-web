LifeMate ecosystem

LifeMate is the core; experiences grow around it.

WellMate and CareMate are the first LifeMate products. They are distinct experiences with one shared idea: care should be simple, respectful and chosen.

WellMate · Personal health

Everyday health without clutter or missed routines.

WellMate is a personal health companion built for daily use, not a complicated dashboard.

01

See your health routines together

Medication, supplements, reminders and daily events live in one flow.

02

Keep daily logging short

Interactions stay simple enough to use every day.

03

Share only if you choose

Your own health experience comes first; trusted sharing is optional.

Women’s health · Part of WellMate

See and log your cycle more easily.

The menstrual calendar is a WellMate feature for cycle logging, calendar review and clearer personal tracking.

01

Log cycle days

Keep cycle information in your own personal experience.

02

Use a clear calendar view

Review logged days without unnecessary complexity.

03

Personal tracking, not medical certainty

This experience does not provide diagnosis, treatment or guaranteed predictions.

This feature is not a substitute for a clinician or medical care.

CareMate · Trusted care

Care without turning support into control.

CareMate is for someone you trust, helping them stay informed at the right time without turning personal boundaries into constant monitoring.

01

Peace of mind

Important, permitted information can be seen when it matters.

02

Timely support

The goal is useful support, not constant surveillance.

03

Clear boundaries

Connections should respect consent, individual choice and minimum necessary access.

How it works

Three steps, always on your terms.

1

Manage your own health

Your daily routines and logs stay in WellMate.

2

Connect someone you trust, if you want

You choose whether the connection exists.

3

Only necessary information flows

CareMate should show only permitted, relevant information.

Privacy & trust

Good care needs clear boundaries.

LifeMate is designed around consent, clarity and individual control. Access should be understandable and limited to what is actually needed.
