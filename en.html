LifeMate, the parent brand

More than two apps: an ecosystem designed to grow with real life.

WellMate and CareMate are the first LifeMate experiences. Each has a distinct job, while both share one promise: health should feel easier to understand and care should remain respectful. The ecosystem can grow without publicly naming ideas that have not launched.

WellMate · Personal Health Companion

Health that fits into everyday life.

WellMate is your personal app for keeping medication, supplements, reminders and a health calendar from getting lost in a busy day.

1

See today clearly

Your treatment plan and daily health tasks, together.

2

Log things without the fuss

Short, clear interactions made for everyday use.

3

You decide what to share

Selected information can be shared with a trusted person.

Women's health · Inside WellMate

Log your cycle and look back on your days with more clarity.

The menstrual calendar is part of WellMate: a private place to log a cycle, view the calendar and revisit information you entered.

Cycle logging Cycle-day review Related notes

This experience supports personal logging and review. It does not replace diagnosis or professional medical advice.

CareMate · Trusted Care

Care, without turning into control.

CareMate is for family, caregivers or someone you trust—helping them stay informed at the right time and only within the boundaries you allow.

1

Peace of mind, not constant monitoring

The goal is timely support, not visibility into every detail.

2

Only what has been allowed

Sharing should be optional, transparent and limited to real need.

3

Support along the treatment journey

Relevant treatment status can be followed within approved access.

Trust & privacy

Good care has clear boundaries.

LifeMate is guided by consent, transparency and minimum necessary access. People should understand what is shared and with whom.

Read our privacy approach
Consent

Connection and sharing begin with a person's choice.

Minimum access

Only what is needed for that kind of support.

Transparency

Boundaries should stay clear and understandable.

Control

Access should remain manageable.
