Part of WellMate

Women’s health, within the same personal WellMate experience.

The menstrual calendar is part of WellMate, helping users log a cycle, view it on a calendar and follow cycle days in a clearer, more consistent way.

CareMate · Trusted care

Care without turning support into control.

CareMate is for someone you trust, so they can be informed at the right time without turning personal boundaries into constant monitoring.

Peace of mind

Important, permitted updates can be seen at the right time.

Timely support

The goal is to help when useful, not to watch constantly.

Individual choice

Connections and sharing levels should begin with the individual’s consent.

Minimum necessary access

Only information needed for support should be visible within approved boundaries.

How it works

A simple connection, on your terms.

1

Manage your own health

Your everyday routines and logs stay in WellMate.

2

Connect someone you trust, if you want

3

Only permitted information flows

CareMate sees only information intended for sharing.
