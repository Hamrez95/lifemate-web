About this page

This page applies to the public LifeMate website. Each application will publish product-specific privacy information based on its final features and infrastructure before public release.

Website data

This version of the website does not provide user accounts or collect health information through forms. If you contact us by email, the information you choose to send is used to respond to that communication.

Product principles

LifeMate is designed around individual consent, clarity, manageable access and minimum necessary access. These are product-design principles and do not replace the final technical and legal documentation for each application.

Health information

LifeMate is not a substitute for a clinician, diagnosis, treatment or emergency services.
