Privacy by design

Privacy is part of the LifeMate design.

This page covers the current public LifeMate website and the principles guiding the design of its health and care experiences.

Last reviewed: August 2026Public websiteNo health-data forms
✓
Short version

The current website does not provide user accounts or collect medical information. The only active contact path is email; information you choose to send by email is handled outside this website for that communication.

Website data

The current site has no signup form, account system, medical-record upload or health-symptom form. Your light/dark theme preference is stored locally in your browser so the display choice can be remembered.

Consent & choice

In LifeMate product experiences, connecting people and sharing information should be based on the individual’s choice and consent. Product-specific implementation details will be published before each product’s public release.

Minimum necessary access

CareMate is not intended to provide broad access to personal information. Access should be limited, as far as practical, to information needed for the relevant support relationship.

Clarity & control

People should be able to understand what information is shared and with whom, and—within the final product capabilities—manage access over time.

Health information

LifeMate and this website are not substitutes for a clinician, diagnosis, treatment or emergency services. The final health-data handling policy will reflect the applications’ production infrastructure and be published before public release.

External services

This website version does not add external analytics, advertising or tracking tools. External links such as LinkedIn or email open in the relevant service after you choose them.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Hamidrezapakpour95@gmail.com. Please avoid sending sensitive medical information through general email unless it is genuinely necessary for your communication.
