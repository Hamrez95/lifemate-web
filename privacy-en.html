Privacy by design

Privacy is part of the LifeMate experience.

This page covers the current public LifeMate website and the principles guiding the design of its health and care experiences.

Last reviewed: August 2026Public websiteNo health-data forms
✓
In short

The current website does not provide user accounts or collect medical information. The only active contact path is email; information you choose to send by email is handled outside this website for that communication.

Website data

The current website has no account signup, medical-record upload or health-symptom form. It is a static introduction to LifeMate and products still in development.

Consent and choice

Within LifeMate products, connecting people and sharing information should begin with the individual's choice and consent. Product-specific implementation details will be explained before public launch.

Minimum necessary access

CareMate is not intended to provide broad access to personal information. Access should be limited, wherever possible, to what is needed for that form of support.

Transparency and control

People should be able to understand what is shared and with whom and, within the final product's capabilities, manage that access.

Health information

LifeMate and this website do not replace a clinician, diagnosis, treatment or emergency services. A detailed policy for health-information processing will be provided before public product release.

External tools

This version does not add behavioral analytics, advertising or third-party trackers. LinkedIn and email links open in their respective services after you choose them.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Hamidrezapakpour95@gmail.com. Please avoid sending sensitive medical details through general email unless they are genuinely needed for your request.
